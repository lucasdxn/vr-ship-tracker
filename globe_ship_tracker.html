Layer downloads
Each layer is fetched the first time you zoom in far enough to need it — nothing extra downloads while you stay at globe scale. The 1:10m coastline is the largest at about 3.5 MB and only loads past 400×.

Vessels shown on land are usually correct positions on a map that is too coarse: even at 1:10m the coastline is around 1 km out, and river ports (Nanjing, Galati, Antwerp) sit on channels that Natural Earth does not cut out of the land at all. Major rivers are drawn as a nominal channel to compensate.
